Meet the four AI companions

Every session covers all four of the major AI tools in plain English, so you know exactly which one to reach for.

ChatGPT — The Friendly All-Rounder

Excels at fast brainstorming, casual phone-like voice conversations, and step-by-step problem solving for everyday tasks.

chatgpt.com

Claude — The Master Writer

Unmatched at human-sounding communication, writing polished emails, summarising lengthy documents, and thoughtful logic.

claude.ai

Gemini — The Live Researcher

Connected directly to live Google Search — perfect for finding real-time facts, reading photos, and connecting to Google Workspace.

gemini.google.com

Copilot — The Office Co-Worker

Works directly inside Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and Teams to organise your existing files, emails and meetings.
