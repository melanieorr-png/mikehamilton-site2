Meet Mike Hamilton

Led From Experience

Leadership looks different when you're the one ultimately accountable.

Mike Hamilton brings the perspective of someone who has led as a CEO and senior executive — making difficult decisions, managing competing expectations, representing organisations publicly and leading people through periods of change, challenge and uncertainty.

He understands that leadership isn't only about strategy, frameworks and management theory. It's also what happens when plans change, pressure builds, difficult conversations need to happen or something goes wrong — and people are looking to you for direction.

That real-world experience shapes Mike's approach to leadership development.

  • • Lead under pressure — staying composed, making decisions and providing direction when circumstances change.
  • • Handle setbacks and difficult moments — learning from the blows that come with leadership and continuing to move people and organisations forward.
  • • Communicate with confidence — with employees, boards, government, industry, stakeholders and the wider community.
  • • Be media ready — preparing properly, staying on message and communicating clearly when representing an organisation publicly.
  • • Lead through change — helping people navigate uncertainty while maintaining trust, accountability and momentum.
  • • Prepare for what's next — anticipating emerging challenges, including workforce change, technology and AI, rather than simply reacting to them.

Mike doesn't teach leadership from the sidelines. He brings the lessons, perspective and practical understanding that come from having been responsible for the decisions, the people and the outcomes.

"You can't prepare for every challenge in leadership. But you can prepare yourself to lead when it arrives."
